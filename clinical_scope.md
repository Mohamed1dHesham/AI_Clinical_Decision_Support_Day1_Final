# Clinical Scope — Day 1

**Topic:** Adult hypertension.

**Included evidence:**
1. NICE guideline: *Hypertension in adults: diagnosis and management (NG136)*.
2. NICE patient decision aid: *How do I control my blood pressure? Lifestyle options and choice of medicines*.

**Question types supported by the current corpus:**
- Measuring and confirming hypertension
- Cardiovascular risk and target-organ assessment
- Lifestyle interventions described in the provided documents
- Blood-pressure medicines and common side effects described in the provided documents
- Monitoring information contained in the provided documents

**Out of scope:** diseases and clinical questions not supported by these two documents; individualized diagnosis; personalized treatment selection; drug dosing; emergency triage beyond what the retrieved documents explicitly support.
